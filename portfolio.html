About

Engineering mindset, built for the field

A practical, problem-first approach to electrical and electronics engineering, grounded in automation, hardware and industrial control, and built on hands-on experience solving real-world engineering challenges.

Profile

Practical, hands-on, automation-focused

I approach engineering with a practical mindset — starting from how a system will actually behave on the factory floor or in the field, not just on paper. Across coursework, training programs and personal projects, I've developed a strong problem-solving ability that comes from repeatedly building, testing, and debugging real hardware and control logic.

My core passion lies in automation: taking a manual or inefficient process and re-engineering it with PLCs, sensors, and embedded controllers. Alongside this, I enjoy hardware development — from prototyping with Arduino and ESP32 to designing PCBs — and I'm equally comfortable working on industrial control systems that need to be robust and safe.

I'm also drawn to renewable energy and smart technology solutions, which is reflected in projects like an inside-pipe hydro power generator and a solar-powered IoT agricultural vehicle — work that pairs electrical engineering fundamentals with sustainability-minded design.
